# ClawPal 0.14.0

- **Audio**, a new app on every board: a volume mixer on your display.
  - A fader and a mute button for your speakers, your microphone, Windows'
    own sounds and every app playing sound, such as Spotify, a browser or
    Discord.
  - Each app shows its own icon, and its fader takes the icon's colour.
  - Drag a fader up or down, or tap anywhere along it to jump there. Moving a
    muted fader up unmutes it.
  - With more apps than fit, the row scrolls sideways.
  - Faders change the same volumes as the Windows volume mixer. The display
    gets each app's name and icon, nothing else.
- Quieter footers: apps no longer show hints along the bottom. Only what
  matters stays, such as the weather's credit to Open-Meteo, a reconnecting
  PC, or why the Clock opened by itself.
- Home no longer names the ClawPal version at the bottom. Turn it back on in
  the PC app under **Home**.
- The timer reminder at the bottom of the screen now sits inside the footer
  instead of running off the edge.
- The Apps menu shows four apps across on 480 × 480 and 320 × 240 displays, so
  all ten fit. Long names use a short form where they don't fit, such as
  Claude for Claude Code.
- Fixed: app icons on Shortcuts tiles were drawn off-centre and cut off.
- Fixed: on some starts of the PC app, Shortcuts' mute tiles never lit up and
  the log filled with "Shortcut state unavailable". The PC app now makes all
  its Windows audio calls one at a time.
- The Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3 (without the B) and the LCDWIKI
  ES3C28P are confirmed working. Choose the 4.3B in the installer for the
  non-B 4.3.
- The PC app and the display need each other's update for Audio; install both.
