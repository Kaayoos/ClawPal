# ClawPal 0.15.0

- **Printer**, a new app for your 3D printers: progress, time left and when the
  print will be done, the current layer, and nozzle and bed temperatures, with
  an animated pixel-art printer.
  - Works with Bambu Lab printers, Klipper (Moonraker), OctoPrint and
    PrusaLink, on your own network. Add printers in the PC app under
    **Apps → Printer → Manage printers**; it looks for printers on your
    network by itself.
  - Read-only: ClawPal never starts, pauses or stops a print and never changes
    anything on the printer. Bambu Lab printers need only the access code from
    their screen, with no Bambu account and no LAN Only or Developer mode.
  - A full-screen alert when a print finishes, fails or pauses, for example
    when the filament runs out. Pausing or cancelling a print yourself stays
    quiet.
  - The app opens by itself while a print is running, and can sit on Home.
- **Synced lyrics** in Media: tap **Lyrics** and each line lights up as it's
  sung, for whatever is playing on your PC.
  - Off by default. Turn on **Show synced lyrics** under **Apps → Media**. The
    lyrics come from LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free community lyrics database,
    which then receives each song's title, artist, album and length.
  - Lines keep time on the display itself, so Wi-Fi delays don't throw them
    off. A timing slider fixes songs that run early or late, for example with
    Bluetooth headphones.
  - Lyrics can also open by themselves when a song has them.
- Choose which apps open by themselves when they start on your PC: Media,
  Codex, Claude Code and Printer each have their own switch under **Apps**.
- Crab v2 and Crastudio can hide their orange case under **Look & language**,
  keeping their colours and shapes.
- New pixel-art pictures: an animated PC beside the CPU and GPU cards in PC
  Monitor, and a picture at the top of every full-screen alert instead of the
  small crab tile.
- The PC app shows a short list of what's new after each update. Find it again
  under **About ClawPal → What's new**.
- A printer that is switched off no longer fills the PC app's log; it is noted
  once, and again when it comes back.
- The Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7 with 16 MB flash is confirmed working.
  The 8 MB version is still experimental.
- The PC app and the display need each other's update; install both. The 0.15.0
  display asks for the 0.15.0 PC app first.
