# ClawPal 0.13.0

- **Shortcuts**, a new app on every board: a grid of tiles on your display that
  run things on your PC.
  - Tiles can mute your microphone or sound, turn the volume down or up (hold
    to keep going), play, pause or skip, lock the PC, turn the screens off,
    show the desktop, take a screenshot or open Task Manager.
  - They can also open an app, a website or a folder, or press any key
    combination, such as a Discord or OBS hotkey.
  - Mute tiles light up while you're muted, play/pause shows what will happen,
    and app tiles show the app's own icon.
  - Every tile says when it worked, or why it didn't.
  - Build the deck in the new **Shortcuts** page of the PC app: pick a 6, 8, 12
    or 16-tile grid, drag tiles into place, and choose each one's action, label,
    icon and colour. **Try it** runs a tile on the PC straight away.
  - Everyone starts with an 8-tile grid: mute mic, mute sound, volume down and
    up, play/pause, next, lock and screenshot. Home stays as it was.
  - What a tile runs stays on your PC. The display only knows how the tile
    looks, and while the PC is locked only sound, media, lock and screens-off
    tiles work.
- The Apps menu now shows five apps across on 800 × 480 displays, so all nine
  fit on one screen.
- A tidier PC app:
  - Settings are grouped under **Display** (Home, Apps, Shortcuts, Clock &
    weather, Screen, Look & language) and **This PC** (PC app, Connection,
    Updates), with each page split into titled sections.
  - Options for a single app sit with that app on the Apps page: game FPS
    under PC Monitor, the Claude Code connection and live limits under Claude
    Code.
  - Add apps to Home by clicking them, instead of picking from a list.
  - Lists scroll with the mouse wheel, jump to an entry as you type, and close
    when you click them again.
  - Setup and Wi-Fi changes list nearby networks: pick yours and type only the
    password. Networks the display can't join (5 GHz only) are marked.
  - Setup has a new look: numbered steps, labelled fields and clearer sections.
    The crab no longer covers the step list when Windows scales text above
    100 %.
- A **Join our Discord** button sits at the bottom of the PC app's sidebar.
- Fixed: displays with FT6336 or FT5x06 touch (the ES3C28P and the M5Stack
  CoreS3) no longer restart in a loop at boot, and keep running without touch if
  the touch chip doesn't answer. Thanks to Skinner2002 for finding and fixing it.
- Fixed: if Windows hadn't finished removing an uninstalled copy of ClawPal,
  the installer stopped with "file not found". It now installs a fresh copy.
- The PC app now refuses to install firmware that isn't signed over USB, since a
  display can't start it.
- The PC app and the display need each other's update for Shortcuts; install
  both.
