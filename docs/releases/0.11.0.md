# ClawPal 0.11.0

- Two looks for the display and the PC app. **Crab** is the playful, animated
  ClawPal you know. **Studio** is calm and refined: graphite surfaces, hairline
  rules, quiet wording, thin Montserrat Light numerals and no mascot. It comes
  with eight accents: Champagne, Sage, Ice, Silver, Rose, Lavender, Copper and
  Teal.
- Setup now starts with your language and look, so the rest of setup already
  wears it. Change it any time under **Appearance** in settings. The display
  restarts for a few seconds to apply a new look, and a running timer starts
  over.
- In Studio, Home's left side is a desk clock: the date, large light numerals
  and the week with today marked. The shortcuts beside it already show what
  each app is doing, so nothing is repeated. Weather shows the condition's own
  mark, and prompts, installs and every app keep their layouts on all three
  screen sizes.
- In both looks, the Apps menu is a grid of large tiles with each app's icon,
  name and what it shows right now. The open app is outlined, and a swipe
  across the grid no longer counts as a tap.
- Music is now **Media**: it follows any player in Windows' media controls and
  has its own play icon. Spotify still gets its logo while it's the one
  playing.
- The Apps icon in the footer now lines up with its label.
- The PC app follows the look, including its accent and wording, and hides the
  crab in Studio.
- Fixed the firmware install screen showing its progress line at the very top
  of the display.
- Display settings moved to schema 7. Update the PC app before the display;
  older PC apps cannot read the new settings.
