ClawPal companion icon

Original artwork created and supplied by MinimalPulse.
Source: assets/mascot/crab_idle_sheet.png and assets/mascot/palette.json.
The icon uses the first 48 x 48 frame of the idle sheet. Painted pixels are
centered by adjusting transparent padding and scaled with nearest-neighbor
sampling. The original colors are retained.

Regenerate: powershell -NoProfile -File companion/Assets/generate-icon.ps1
Outputs: crab-tray.png and BuddyOS.ico (the legacy filename is internal).
